Privacy Policy

AS LEGENDS 2

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how EZLABS collects, uses and protects information when you play AS Legends 2. You play as a guest: the Game does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, date of birth or payment details, and it contains no advertising, no in-app purchases, no player chat and no third-party analytics or tracking.

Article 1. Introduction

1. AS Legends 2 (the "Game") is published and operated by EZLABS Technology Company Limited and its subsidiaries and affiliates (together, "EZLABS", "we", "us" or "our"). We take our data protection responsibilities seriously and respect the privacy of every player.

2. "Services" means the Game, its game servers and the support channels that serve it.

3. "Personal Data" means data from which an individual can be identified, alone or together with other information we hold or are likely to have access to.

4. By using the Services you confirm that you have read this Policy. If you do not agree with it, please do not use the Services.

Article 2. When we collect data

We collect data:

  • when you open the Game for the first time and it creates your guest account;
  • while you play: creating a character, playing matches, completing quests, raising pets and using items;
  • when you contact us for support or send us feedback or a complaint.

Article 3. What data we collect

1. Guest identifier. The first time you open the Game, it creates a random identifier and stores it on your device. The identifier is sent to our server, together with the device platform (for example "iPhone"), to create and sign in to your guest account. It is not your Apple ID, not the advertising identifier (IDFA), and cannot identify you outside the Game.

2. Game data linked to your guest account:

  • the character you create: its name, class and appearance;
  • your progress: level, experience, in-game Gold, items, equipment, skills, pets, quests and rankings;
  • match records: game mode, map, team, score, kills and results.

3. Support data. If you contact us, we receive what you include in your message, such as your email address and a description of the problem.

4. What we do not collect. We do not collect your name, email address (unless you write to us), phone number, date of birth, identity documents, contacts, photos, precise or approximate location, microphone or camera data, or payment information.

Article 4. Other data collected automatically

When you connect to our servers, they record the time you sign in and out, your daily play time, the IP address of the connection, and a history of virtual items and Gold received and spent. We use these records to run the Services, keep them secure, investigate cheating and answer support requests.

Article 5. Cookies and similar technologies

The Game does not use cookies, advertising identifiers or similar tracking technologies, and it does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies. This policy website does not set cookies.

Article 6. How we use data

  • to create your guest account and keep your progress;
  • to run matches, including matches with computer-controlled players (bots);
  • to show your character name, class, level and results to other players in rooms, matches and rankings;
  • to keep the Game fair and secure, and to detect and act on cheating or abuse;
  • to fix problems, improve the Game and answer support requests;
  • to comply with the law and with requests from competent authorities.

We do not use your data for advertising or marketing, and we do not sell it.

Article 7. How we protect and store data

1. We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections, access controls on our servers and administrator tools, and logging of administrative actions. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

2. Retention:

  • your account and game data are kept while you use the Game and for at least 6 months after you stop;
  • sign-in history, play sessions, daily play time, virtual item history and administrator logs are kept for 24 months; records older than 24 months are deleted automatically every day.

Article 8. Disclosure of data

We disclose data only:

  • to other players, limited to what the Game displays (character name, class, level, team and match results);
  • to service providers that host our servers on our behalf, under confidentiality obligations;
  • to competent authorities when the law or a court order requires it;
  • to a successor company if the Game is transferred, under the terms of this Policy.

Article 9. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 (under 16 in the European Union), and we do not knowingly collect Personal Data from them. Because the Game does not ask for Personal Data, a child's guest account holds only the data described in Article 3. If you believe a child has provided us with Personal Data, contact us and we will delete it.

Article 10. Third-party services

The Game does not include third-party advertising, analytics, social login or payment services. Apple distributes the Game through the App Store; Apple's handling of your data is governed by Apple's own privacy policy.

Article 11. Third-party websites

This Policy may link to websites that we do not operate. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites, and we encourage you to read their privacy policies.

Article 12. Your choices and rights

1. Delete your account. You can delete your account at any time in the Game: Settings → Delete account. When you confirm, we immediately erase the guest identifier and the IP addresses stored on your account, release its character name and lock the account, so it can no longer be signed in to or linked to your device. The Game then starts over with a new account.

2. Some game records (for example your character name in past match results and rankings, and the server logs in Article 7, which can include IP addresses) may remain for the retention periods above, no longer linked to any identifier of yours. To ask for these records to be erased as well, contact us (Article 15).

3. Because a guest account is tied to the identifier stored on your device, deleting the Game or resetting your device may make your account impossible to recover.

4. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to access, correct or receive a copy of your data, and to restrict or object to its processing. Contact us to exercise these rights.

Article 13. Additional terms for users in the European Union and the United Kingdom

1. EZLABS is the controller of your Personal Data.

2. We process your data on these legal bases: to perform our agreement with you (running your account and the Game); our legitimate interests (keeping the Game fair and secure, fixing problems, handling support requests); and compliance with legal obligations.

3. You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the UK GDPR. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Article 14. Where data is stored and transferred

Your data is stored on servers located in Vietnam, in a database that only the game servers can access. If you play from outside Vietnam, including from the European Union or the United Kingdom, your data is transferred to Vietnam. We protect it as described in this Policy wherever it is processed.

Article 15. Questions, concerns or complaints? Contact EZLABS

If you have any question, concern or complaint about this Policy or your data, contact us at privacy.ezlabs@gmail.com.

We may update this Policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed. If the changes are significant, we will tell you in the Game before they take effect.
