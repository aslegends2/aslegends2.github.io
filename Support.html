Contact us

To help us find your account quickly, please include:

  • your Player ID, shown at the bottom of Settings in the Game;
  • your device model and iOS version;
  • the game version, also shown in Settings;
  • a short description of the problem, with a screenshot if possible.

Email: privacy.ezlabs@gmail.com

Delete your account

You can delete your account directly in the Game:

  • open Settings;
  • tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is locked at once and its personal data is erased. Deletion is permanent: your progress and items cannot be restored. The Game then starts over with a new account. See the Privacy Policy for details on what is kept and for how long.

If you can no longer open the Game, email us your Player ID and we will delete the account for you.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to sign up or log in?
No. The Game creates a guest account for your device the first time you open it.

I reinstalled the Game and my progress is gone.
A guest account is tied to an identifier stored on your device. Deleting the Game, resetting your device or moving to a new device can make the old account impossible to recover. If this happens, email us your old Player ID if you have it and we will check what we can do.

Are the other players real?
Matches can include computer-controlled players (bots) that fill empty slots, so you can always start a match quickly.

Does the Game have purchases or ads?
No. The Game has no in-app purchases and no advertising.

The Game cannot connect.
Check your internet connection, then close and reopen the Game. If the problem continues, email us with the time it happened and your network type (Wi-Fi or mobile data).

How do I report a cheater or a bug?
Email us with the player's name, the match time and a screenshot or video if you have one.
